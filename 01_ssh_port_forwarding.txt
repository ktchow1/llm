**********************************************
*** ssh port local-forwarding (in general) ***
**********************************************
What is local-forwarding in ssh?
* method 1     cmd > ssh -N -L [ssh_client_port] : [service_addr] : [service_port] [ssh_user]@[ssh_server_addr]
* method 2     cmd > ssh <ssh_server_alias>                          plus following .ssh/config in ssh_client

  Host <ssh_server_alias>
    HostName      <ssh_server_addr>
    User          <ssh_user>
    LocalForward  <ssh_client_port> <service_addr>:<service_port>

ssh-tunnel setup steps :
* ssh_server listens to its own port 22
* ssh_client connects to ssh_server port 22
* ssh-tunnel's port on ssh_server side is assigned by ssh_server's OS
* ssh-tunnel's port on ssh_client side is assigned by ssh_client's OS
* ssh_server keeps listening to port 22 for future request from other clients

Local forwarding steps :
* ssh_client listens to its own ssh_client_port
* ssh_server listens to ssh-tunnel
* ssh_client forwards all traffic from ssh_client_port to ssh-tunnel
* ssh_server forwards all traffic from ssh-tunnel to "service_addr : service_port" via ordinary TCP connection

General use case (3 roles) :

            ssh              tcp
ssh_client <---> ssh_server <---> service_host

Specific use case (2 roles) :

            ssh
ssh_client <---> ssh_server == service_host




***********************************************
*** ssh port remote-forwarding (in general) ***
***********************************************
What is remote-forwarding in ssh?
* method 1     cmd > ssh -N -R [ssh_server_port] : [service_addr] : [service_port] [ssh_user]@[ssh_server_addr]
* method 2     cmd > ssh <ssh_server_alias>                          plus following .ssh/config in ssh_client

  Host <ssh_server_alias>
    HostName       <ssh_server_addr>
    User           <ssh_user>
    RemoteForward  <ssh_server_port> <service_addr>:<service_port>

ssh-tunnel setup steps (exactly same as that in local-forwarding) :
* ssh_server listens to its own port 22
* ssh_client connects to ssh_server port 22
* ssh-tunnel's port on ssh_server side is assigned by ssh_server's OS
* ssh-tunnel's port on ssh_client side is assigned by ssh_client's OS
* ssh_server keeps listening to port 22 for future request from other clients

Remote forwarding steps :
* ssh_server listens to its own ssh_server_port
* ssh_client listens to ssh-tunnel
* ssh_server forwards all traffic from ssh_server_port to ssh-tunnel
* ssh_client forwards all traffic from ssh-tunnel to "service_addr : service_port" via ordinary TCP connection

General use case (3 roles) :

            ssh              tcp
ssh_server <---> ssh_client <---> service_host

Specific use case (2 roles) :

            ssh
ssh_server <---> ssh_client == service_host




**************
*** Remark ***
**************
1. ssh -N means no remote command, just setup port-forwarding
2. ssh-tunnel is TCP connection with extra security
3. ssh_server is sometimes known as jump-host in both "ssh -L" and "ssh -R" case (but why?)
4. for both local and remote forwarding, both ssh_client and ssh_server are involved in forwarding
